# Week 6 Subgroup Summary

In week 6 the team created separate data stories for different UrbanStyle locations and channels.

## Subgroups

- Tallinn store
- Tartu store
- Parnu store
- Online store

Each subgroup created a dashboard view, added annotations, and wrote a short business story.

## Main Conclusion

UrbanStyle locations should not all be managed in the same way. Each location has its own sales pattern, customer behavior, and possible risks.

## What We Learned

Data storytelling helps turn charts into decisions. A dashboard should explain what happened, why it matters, and what should be done next.
