## Subtask A: Tallinn Store Story

Tallinn is UrbanStyle's headquarters location and largest store. Therefore, the goal of the Tallinn view is to show what makes this location strong and which working patterns could also be used to develop other stores.

The dashboard needed to answer these questions:

- how strong Tallinn's total revenue is;
- how Tallinn's sales change by month;
- which products or categories bring the most sales;
- which customer segments are most important for Tallinn;
- what recommendation follows from the data.

- I wrote a short data story and business recommendation for the Tallinn dashboard.

## Design Decisions

I placed the most important KPI cards at the top of the dashboard because a manager needs to see the overview first. For the sales trend, I used a line chart because it is best suited for showing change over time. For comparing products or categories, I used a bar chart because it makes it easy to quickly notice what drives sales the most.

The purpose of the annotations was to highlight not only a large number, but also its meaning. For example, if a month or category stands out strongly, the dashboard must explain why it matters for management.

For colors and style, I followed the earlier UrbanStyle dashboard design: calm background, clear titles, highlighted KPIs, and a limited number of accent colors.

## Data Story

Tallinn is UrbanStyle's largest and most important physical store. The dashboard shows which sales periods, products, and customer segments contribute the most to Tallinn's result. This means that Tallinn can be a benchmark for the company when evaluating development opportunities for other locations. The recommendation is to keep stock levels sufficient in Tallinn's strongest categories and use Tallinn's successful campaign and product selection patterns in other stores as well.

## Executive Summary

- Tallinn is UrbanStyle's main physical sales location and therefore an important benchmark for other locations.
- The sales trend helps show whether Tallinn's strong result is stable or depends on individual campaigns.
- The TOP products or categories view shows which product groups drive Tallinn's sales the most.
- Customer segment analysis helps understand who the Tallinn store works best for.
- The recommendation is to use Tallinn's strong patterns to improve other locations, but first check whether the same pattern fits the local customer.

## AI Usage

I used AI to refine the wording of the data story and annotations. AI helped make the conclusions shorter and more understandable for management, but I checked that the final text was based on the results visible in the Power BI dashboard.

## Topics Learned

- A location-based filter makes the same dataset much more specific and easier to interpret.
- KPI cards work well for a quick management overview.
- A line chart helps show whether sales are growing, falling, or fluctuating.
- An annotation must answer the question "So what?", not only repeat the number shown in the chart.
- A reference line gives context to the result and helps understand whether the result is expected.
- The value of a dashboard increases when a clear recommendation is added to it.
