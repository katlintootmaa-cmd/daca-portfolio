## What I Did

This week I learned SQL aggregation and practiced how to turn single data rows into summary indicators. I focused on `GROUP BY`, `HAVING`, CTEs, and window functions.

## Activities

- Practiced grouping sales by month, city, and category.
- Used `COUNT()`, `SUM()`, `AVG()`, `MIN()`, and `MAX()`.
- Learned the difference between `WHERE` and `HAVING`.
- Wrote clearer SQL queries with CTEs.
- Tried window functions such as `LAG()`, `ROW_NUMBER()`, and `SUM() OVER (...)`.
- Used the results to create a short business interpretation.

## What I Learned

I learned that aggregation is useful when we need summaries instead of raw rows. I also learned that SQL analysis becomes more valuable when the results are connected to business questions.

## Summary

By the end of the individual work, I could use SQL aggregation to find sales trends, compare customer groups, and understand inventory better.
