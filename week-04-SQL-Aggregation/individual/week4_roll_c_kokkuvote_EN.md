# Week 4 Individual Summary: Role C

My individual role was Role C, which focused on inventory statistics.

## What I Did

I used SQL aggregation to compare product sales, stock levels, and category performance. The goal was to understand which products or categories sell well and where inventory risks may appear.

## SQL Skills Used

- `GROUP BY`
- `COUNT()`
- `SUM()`
- `AVG()`
- `MIN()` and `MAX()`
- `HAVING`
- CTEs with `WITH`
- joins between product, sales, and inventory data

## What I Learned

I learned that inventory analysis is not only about counting stock. It also needs sales context. A product can have stock, but if it does not sell, the business needs a different decision than for a product that sells quickly.

## Summary

This task helped me understand how SQL summaries can support stock and product decisions.
