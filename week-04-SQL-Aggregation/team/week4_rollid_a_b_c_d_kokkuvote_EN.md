# Week 4 Team Role Summary

This file summarizes the Week 4 team roles for SQL aggregation.

## Role A: Sales Summary

Role A focused on sales by month and category. The goal was to find revenue trends, number of orders, and average order value.

## Role B: Customer Groups

Role B focused on customer segmentation. Customers were grouped into useful business groups such as VIP, regular, or new customers.

## Role C: Inventory Statistics

Role C compared inventory, sales, and product profitability. This helped show which categories or products may need more attention.

## Role D: Marketing Performance

Role D focused on marketing campaign performance and ROI. The goal was to compare channels and understand which campaigns created value.

## Main Learning

Aggregation helps turn many rows into clear summary numbers. These numbers become useful when they are connected to business interpretation and recommendations.
