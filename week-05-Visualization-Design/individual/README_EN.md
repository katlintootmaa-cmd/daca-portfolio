## What I Did

During this week's individual work, I completed subtask D, the UrbanStyle investor summary view. My task was to create a dashboard that summarizes the company's most important indicators and helps an investor quickly understand whether UrbanStyle is a growing and investable company.

The focus was not only on creating charts, but also on presenting a clear business story. An investor does not need every detail separately, but a short and reliable overview: how large sales are, whether revenue is growing, which categories and channels are strong, and where there may be risks.

## Subtask D: Investor Summary View

The goal of subtask D was to bring together the most important findings from the CEO, marketing, and operations views into one clear dashboard. The dashboard needed to answer the question: is UrbanStyle investable?

In my view, the following metrics were important:

- total revenue;
- number of customers;
- average order value;
- yearly growth;
- revenue trend;
- strongest product categories;
- most important sales channels and stores.

## Design Decisions

When creating the dashboard, I started from the idea that an investor should understand the main message as quickly as possible. For this reason, I placed the most important KPIs at the top of the view and the more detailed charts below them.

I used KPI cards for individual key indicators, a line chart to show growth and trend, and bar charts to compare categories and channels. I avoided unnecessary elements so that the dashboard would not become too busy or difficult to read.

For the color palette, I used a style that matched the UrbanStyle brand. The goal was to keep the view professional, clear, and suitable for a board or investor audience.

## Business Conclusion

Based on the dashboard, UrbanStyle has a positive growth picture. Revenue in 2024 grew by 19.08% compared with 2023, which shows that the company has meaningful growth potential for an investor.

The strongest categories were shoes, men's clothing, and women's clothing. Among sales channels and stores, Tallinn and Online sales stood out as important. This means that UrbanStyle should maintain stock levels in the strongest categories and continue developing the online channel, because it gives the company a good opportunity to grow without relying only on the expansion of physical stores.

## AI Usage

I used AI to check which chart types are best suited for an investor summary view. AI also helped formulate the business conclusion so that it would not only describe the numbers, but also highlight a recommendation that supports decision-making.

## Topics Learned

- An investor view must be short, clear, and focused on decisions.
- High-level KPIs help quickly show whether the company is growing and what condition it is in.
- The structure of a dashboard must be based on the viewer's needs, not only on the available data.
- A line chart works well for showing trends, and a bar chart works well for comparing categories or channels.
- A good visualization always needs interpretation, because business value is created when the numbers are connected to a recommendation.
