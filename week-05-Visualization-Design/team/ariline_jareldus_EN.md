# Week 5 Business Conclusion

The Week 5 dashboard work showed that UrbanStyle had a positive growth picture.

Revenue in 2024 grew by 19.08% compared with 2023. This is a good sign for the investor view because it shows that the company has growth potential.

The strongest product categories were shoes, men's clothing, and women's clothing. Tallinn and Online sales were also important for the overall result.

## Recommendation

UrbanStyle should keep enough stock in the strongest categories and continue developing the online channel. Online sales can help the company grow without relying only on physical store expansion.

## Risk

One important risk is inventory management. If the strongest categories do not have enough stock, UrbanStyle may lose sales opportunities.
