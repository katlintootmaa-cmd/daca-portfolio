# Week 3 Team Role Summaries

This file summarizes the Week 3 team roles for SQL JOIN work.

## Role A: Sales and Customers

This role focused on connecting sales with customer data. The goal was to understand who bought products and which customers created the most revenue.

## Role C: Unsold Products and Inventory

This role focused on finding products that had not been sold. `LEFT JOIN` was useful because it helped keep product rows even when no matching sales row existed.

## Role D: Sales Channels

This role focused on comparing sales channels. The goal was to understand which channels brought more sales and customers.

## Main Learning

SQL JOINs help combine separate tables into one useful business view. They are especially useful when we need to find both matching records and missing relationships.
