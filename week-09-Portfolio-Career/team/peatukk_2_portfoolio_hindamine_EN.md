# Chapter 2: Evaluating the Portfolio

This chapter explains how to evaluate a candidate's data portfolio.

## What to Look For

- clear project README files
- explanation of the business problem
- description of the data and tools
- SQL, Python, or dashboard examples
- results and conclusions
- business recommendations

## Strong Portfolio

A strong portfolio tells a clear story. It shows what problem was solved, what the candidate did, and what value the result created.

## Weak Portfolio

A weak portfolio contains files without explanation, unclear charts, or code without a business conclusion.

## Main Idea

A portfolio should help an employer quickly understand what the candidate can do.
