# Chapter 4: 30-60-90 Day Onboarding Plan

This chapter describes a possible onboarding plan for a junior data analyst.

## First 30 Days

The analyst learns the company, data sources, dashboards, business goals, and team workflow.

## Days 30-60

The analyst starts doing smaller analysis tasks, checks data quality, updates dashboards, and asks questions from stakeholders.

## Days 60-90

The analyst takes ownership of a small analysis project and presents findings with a clear recommendation.

## Main Idea

A good onboarding plan helps the new analyst move from learning to independent work step by step.
