# Chapter 5: Writing the Job Offer

This chapter explains how to write a clear job offer for a junior data analyst role.

## What the Job Offer Should Include

- role purpose
- main tasks
- required tools and skills
- expected projects
- team and business context
- portfolio expectations
- learning opportunities

## Good Job Offer

A good job offer is specific and realistic. It explains what the analyst will actually do and what skills are most important.

## Weak Job Offer

A weak job offer is too general, asks for too many senior skills, or does not explain the business context.

## Main Idea

The job offer should help the right junior candidate understand the role and decide whether to apply.
