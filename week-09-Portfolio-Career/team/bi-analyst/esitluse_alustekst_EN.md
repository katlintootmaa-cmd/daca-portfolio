# Presentation Opening Text: BI Analyst

Hello. Our presentation is about the BI Analyst role at UrbanStyle.

A BI Analyst creates dashboards and reports that help managers and teams understand performance. The role needs SQL, Power BI, KPI logic, data modeling, and visualization design.

Our hiring guide explains how to evaluate dashboards, technical skills, portfolio, onboarding, and interview answers.

The main message is that a good BI Analyst makes data easy to use for decisions.
