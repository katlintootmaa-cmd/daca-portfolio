# Presentation Opening Text

Hello. Today we are presenting our Week 9 team work about career preparation and the employer's point of view.

Our task was to create a hiring guide for an UrbanStyle data analyst role. We looked at what an employer should evaluate when hiring a junior data analyst.

The guide includes technical skills, portfolio review, CV and LinkedIn review, a 30-60-90 day onboarding plan, a job offer, and an interview scenario.

The main idea is that a strong candidate does not only list tools. They show real projects, explain results clearly, and connect data analysis with business decisions.
