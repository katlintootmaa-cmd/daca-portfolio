# Team Work: UrbanStyle Career Preparation

This team work focused on preparing a hiring guide for an UrbanStyle data analyst role.

## Goal

The goal was to think from the employer's point of view and define how a junior data analyst candidate should be evaluated.

## Main Parts

- technical skills evaluation
- portfolio evaluation
- CV and LinkedIn evaluation
- 30-60-90 day onboarding plan
- job offer writing
- interview scenario

## What We Learned

We learned that an employer looks for evidence, not only a list of skills. A strong candidate should show projects, results, communication skills, and the ability to connect data with business questions.

## Summary

This work helped us understand what makes a junior data analyst candidate stronger and how to present our own portfolio better.
