# Chapter 1: Evaluating Technical Skills

This chapter explains how to evaluate the technical skills of a junior data analyst candidate.

## What to Check

- SQL basics and JOINs
- data cleaning and data quality checks
- aggregation and business summaries
- Python or pandas basics
- dashboard and visualization skills
- ability to explain results

## Strong Candidate

A strong candidate can show practical project work, explain their code or query, check data quality, and connect the result to a business question.

## Weak Candidate

A weak candidate only lists tools, gives unclear answers, and cannot explain how the analysis supports a real decision.

## Main Idea

Technical skills are stronger when the candidate can show evidence from projects, not only say that they know a tool.
