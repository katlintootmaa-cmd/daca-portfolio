# Presentation Opening Text: Data Analyst

Hello. Our presentation is about the Data Analyst role at UrbanStyle.

A Data Analyst helps the company understand sales, customers, products, and performance through data. The role requires SQL, data cleaning, dashboards, and clear explanations.

In our hiring guide, we explain how to evaluate technical skills, portfolio, CV, LinkedIn, onboarding, job offer, and interview answers for this role.

The main message is that a good Data Analyst can turn data into clear business insight.
