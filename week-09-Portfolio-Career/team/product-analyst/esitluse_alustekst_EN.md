# Presentation Opening Text: Product Analyst

Hello. Our presentation is about the Product Analyst role at UrbanStyle.

A Product Analyst studies user behavior, funnels, conversion, retention, and product metrics. The goal is to help improve the product or online store based on data.

Our hiring guide explains how to evaluate whether a candidate can create hypotheses, choose useful metrics, and recommend measurable product improvements.

The main message is that a good Product Analyst connects user behavior with product decisions.
