# Chapter 6: Interview Scenario

This chapter describes a possible interview structure for a junior data analyst candidate.

## Interview Structure

- introduction and candidate background
- portfolio walkthrough
- technical questions
- practical business case
- STAR-method behavioral questions
- candidate questions
- next steps

## Example Case

The candidate could be asked to analyze a simple sales or customer problem and explain what data they would check, what query or dashboard they would build, and what recommendation they would give.

## What to Evaluate

- technical thinking
- data quality awareness
- business interpretation
- communication
- learning ability

## Main Idea

The interview should test not only tool knowledge, but also how the candidate thinks and explains their work.
