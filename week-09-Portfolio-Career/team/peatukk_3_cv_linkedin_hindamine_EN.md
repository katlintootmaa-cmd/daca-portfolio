# Chapter 3: Evaluating CV and LinkedIn

This chapter explains how to evaluate a candidate's CV and LinkedIn profile.

## CV

A good CV should be short, clear, and connected to the role. It should include technical skills, projects, tools, and measurable results.

## LinkedIn

A good LinkedIn profile should show the target role, main skills, and best portfolio projects. The headline should be clear, for example: Junior Data Analyst | SQL, Python, Power BI.

## Strong Candidate

A strong candidate gives concrete examples and results. They show where they used SQL, Python, Power BI, or other tools.

## Weak Candidate

A weak candidate uses general words and lists tools without showing project evidence.

## Main Idea

CV and LinkedIn should tell the same clear story as the portfolio.
