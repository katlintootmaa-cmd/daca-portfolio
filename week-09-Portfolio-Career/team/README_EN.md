# Week 9: Team Role-Based Work

Eestikeelne versioon: [README.md](README.md)

This folder contains the Week 9 team work divided by analyst roles. Each role has its own folder and a complete `README.md` file.

## Role Split

| Role | Team member | Responsibility |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| A | Ragnar | Hiring manager view: CV, LinkedIn, portfolio fit, and hiring recommendation. |
| B | Karmo | Technical interviewer view: GitHub, code, SQL, Python, visualization, and technical reasoning. |
| C | Mari | HR or people manager view: communication, soft skills, learning ability, and team fit. |
| D | Katlin | Team lead view: independence, documentation, collaboration, and growth potential. |

## Role Descriptions

- Role A evaluates whether the candidate's public profile tells a clear and convincing story for the target analyst role.
- Role B evaluates whether the candidate has practical technical evidence, not only a list of tools.
- Role C evaluates whether the candidate can communicate, learn, receive feedback, and work with others.
- Role D evaluates whether the candidate would be effective in daily team work and could grow in the role.
