# Week 10 Team Work: Portfolio Deploy

Eestikeelne versioon: [README.md](README.md)

## Summary

Week 10 focuses on finalizing, publishing, and checking the portfolio. The team's goal is to make sure that the structure, links, documentation, and presentation readiness of the work support job search or final portfolio use.

## Role Distribution

| Role | Team Member | Task |
|------|-------------|------|
| A | Kätlin | Portfolio quality control: checked README structure, weekly descriptions, and main evidence materials. |
| B | Ragnar | Deploy and links: checked publishing, access, broken links, and technical usability. |
| C | Karmo | Technical project review: reviewed code, SQL/Python outputs, and reproducibility. |
| D | Mari | Presentation and business message: formulated the portfolio's main value proposition and polished summaries. |

## Role Descriptions

- Role A, the portfolio quality reviewer, was responsible for content completeness. The role checked that the weekly work had clear READMEs, an understandable structure, and enough evidence of completed work.
- Role B, the deploy and link checker, was responsible for public usability. The role checked whether links open, files are accessible, and the portfolio's technical presentation does not prevent the evaluator from seeing the work.
- Role C, the technical reviewer, was responsible for project reproducibility. The role checked whether SQL, Python, notebooks, or pipelines are logical, documented, and runnable again if needed.
- Role D, the presentation story writer, was responsible for the portfolio's business message. The role described what the portfolio shows about the candidate's skills and how to explain it briefly to an evaluator or employer.

## Output

A finished portfolio with a clear structure, working links, an understandable week-by-week development story, and a short business summary.
